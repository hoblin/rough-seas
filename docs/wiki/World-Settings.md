Rough Seas works in every world it is added to: the wind, [storms](Storms), [currents](Currents) and the [condition of every hull](Hull-Condition) come with the mod, whatever the world was made with. Two things on the new world screen decide how much sea there is and whether it can hurt you.

## The Rough Seas playstyle

Since Vintage Story 1.21 a new world is mostly land, with only a few percent of ocean. The *Rough Seas* playstyle on the new world screen makes a world with far more open water: it starts from the survival playstyle and lowers the land cover to 60%. It also sets 100,000 blocks from pole to equator, twice the default, so the climate zones are twice as wide and a block of open water counts as 100 m of real fetch instead of 200 ([the world's scale](Wind-and-Waves#the-worlds-scale)). The rest is a plain survival world with harsh winters and realistic climate, so the latitude decides the [wind](Wind-and-Waves) and the [storms](Storms).

A world made with any other playstyle still gets every mechanic of the mod and every [vessel](Vessels); it simply has less sea to cross.

## Dangerous oceans

A world setting, on by default. It decides whether the sea can harm your vessels. It can be changed after the world is made, with `/worldconfig dangerousOceans false`.

With it off, the sea keeps its weather but does no harm:

- no [waves](Surf-and-Swamping) come aboard, so no water is shipped, no breaker strikes a hull and nothing [capsizes](Surf-and-Swamping#capsizing);
- no rain falls into a [bilge](Bailing);
- storms bring no extra [lightning](Lightning), masts draw none, and weather does no damage to a hull;
- a mooring takes no strain from the sea, so no [anchor](Anchoring) drags and no [rope snaps](Mooring#tying-up), and no vessel is [thrown ashore](Mooring#thrown-ashore) while you are away.

What stays either way:

- wind, [storms](Storms), rain and their warnings;
- [currents](Currents) and the drift of a vessel left alone;
- [impacts](Hull-Condition#knocks-and-landings) on rock and shore, [rams](Hull-Condition#rams) between vessels, [rapids](Rapids), weapons and explosions, a [currach's seams and a reed boat's soaking](Hull-Condition#seeping-and-soaking);
- [mending](Mending), [bailing](Bailing), anchoring and tying up.

A swimmer breathes by the waves you see either way: a crest over your head takes your air, and a trough gives it back. Drowning is the game's own; the setting doesn't change it. Cold water and swimming stamina are planned for [The living sea](Roadmap#the-living-sea).
