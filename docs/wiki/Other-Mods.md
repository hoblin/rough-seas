Rough Seas drives the game's own weather rather than replacing it, so most mods built for plain Vintage Story work beside it with nothing to do (see [For modders](For-Modders)). These are the mods we have tested it with, on Vintage Story 1.22.7.

| Mod | Version | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Dynamic Seasonal Temperature](https://mods.vintagestory.at/show/mod/69562) | 1.4.0 | Compatible. |
| [Better and Improved Weather Continued](https://mods.vintagestory.at/biwc) | 2.0.5 | Compatible. |
| [Realistic Water](https://mods.vintagestory.at/realisticwater) | 0.4.0 | Compatible. |
| [Realistic Rapids](https://mods.vintagestory.at/realisticrapids) | 0.1.3 | Compatible. |
| [Extreme Weathers](https://mods.vintagestory.at/show/mod/58501) | 1.8.5 | Compatible, with changes on both sides: [Extreme Weathers](Extreme-Weathers). |
| [Terra Prety](https://mods.vintagestory.at/terraprety) | 7.11.2-pre.1 | Under work, no verdict yet. |
| [Wildcraft: Trees and Shrubs](https://mods.vintagestory.at/wildcrafttree) | 1.3.4 | Works, but its woods don't make our vessels yet. They will with [#95](https://github.com/hoblin/rough-seas/issues/95). |

Running Rough Seas with a mod not listed here? Tell us how it went on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/hoblin/rough-seas/issues).
