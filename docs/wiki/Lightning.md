Warm [storms](Storms) bring thunder, and Rough Seas adds lightning strikes inside them on top of the game's own. A strike can split a hull, and a mast draws it.

## Where it strikes

Wherever a storm is at least half its full strength and the air is at least 12 °C, lightning falls within 200 blocks of you, about twice a minute under a storm at its peak and less often towards its edges. In colder air there are none, so a cold gale brings no strikes.

## Masts draw it

Any strike that lands within a mast's height of a masted vessel jumps to the top of the mast, the game's own lightning as well as the storm's. Only two vessels carry a mast:

- the [sailboat](Sailboat), whose 6 m mast draws strikes from 6 blocks around;
- the [outrigger canoe](Outrigger-Canoe), whose 3 m mast draws them from 3 blocks around.

A mast is a lightning rod with you at its foot. Unmasted boats are struck only by bad luck.

## What it does to a hull

The game's lightning harms every creature within 8 blocks of where it lands, and a vessel counts. Rough Seas turns that harm into a [strike on the hull](Hull-Condition#how-a-hull-takes-a-blow) worth 6000 joules. What happens next depends on what the hull is made of:

| Vessel (oak where it matters) | What a strike does |
| --- | --- |
| [Sailboat](Sailboat) | scratches a place |
| [Dugout](Dugout), [outrigger canoe](Outrigger-Canoe) | scratches a place |
| [Hide currach](Hide-Currach), [birch-bark canoe](Birch-Bark-Canoe), [raft](Raft), [coracle](Coracle) | breaks it up |
| [Reed boat](Reed-Boat) | tears it apart |

The players aboard take the game's own lightning damage as well.

With [Dangerous oceans](World-Settings#dangerous-oceans) off, storms bring no extra strikes, masts draw none, and lightning does no harm to a hull.
