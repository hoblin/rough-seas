The mountain streams of Vintage Story 1.22 run down in white water. Every second a hull spends in it, the rocks strike its bottom with 300 joules, a hard knock, again and again.

**Rapids are a simplified model for now.** Every hull but the bark canoe takes the same strike each second, whatever it weighs and however little water it draws. Real coracles shot rapids, floating in a few inches of water, but here the [coracle](Coracle) and the [hide currach](Hide-Currach) don't survive them: their first rock breaks the lath under the hide. Keep them out of white water. Rapids worked out from how deep a hull draws, how heavy it is and how fast the river runs are planned.

Every strike cracks a boat's bottom further, and a cracked bottom [leaks](Hull-Condition#leaks).

## The canoe that runs them

The [birch-bark canoe](Birch-Bark-Canoe) is the only boat that runs rapids unharmed. It draws almost no water and rides over the rocks that batter everything else, as the canoes of the northern woodlands did on the rivers they were built for. Where a river runs too wild even so, pick the canoe up and carry it around.

Heavy hulls, the [dugout](Dugout) and the [outrigger](Outrigger-Canoe), shrug off a few seconds of white water but not a long run. Light ones break within seconds: the [raft](Raft) comes apart, and the [coracle](Coracle) and the [currach](Hide-Currach) have the lath under their hide broken by the first rock.

Rapids harm a hull with [Dangerous oceans](World-Settings#dangerous-oceans) on or off. How the strikes are weighed is on [Hull condition](Hull-Condition#how-a-hull-takes-a-blow).
