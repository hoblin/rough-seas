The mountain streams of Vintage Story 1.22 run down in white water. Every second a hull spends in it, the rocks strike its bottom with 300 joules, a hard knock, again and again.

**Rapids are a simplified model for now.** The two boats that draw only a few inches, the bark canoe and the coracle, run them unharmed, and every other hull takes the same strike each second, whatever it weighs and however little water it draws. The [hide currach](Hide-Currach) doesn't survive them: its first rock breaks the lath under its hide. Keep it out of white water. Rapids worked out from how deep a hull draws, how heavy it is and how fast the river runs are planned.

| Vessel, in oak | Each second | Until |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Hide currach](Hide-Currach) | a lath under the bottom broken through | the bottom is stove in at the first strike |
| [Raft](Raft) | a quarter of its lashings | it comes apart in about four seconds |
| [Dugout](Dugout), [outrigger canoe](Outrigger-Canoe) | a hundredth | about a minute and a quarter |
| [Sailboat](Sailboat) | less | nearly two minutes |
| [Reed boat](Reed-Boat) | nothing: its bundles crush and take it | |
| [Birch-bark canoe](Birch-Bark-Canoe), [coracle](Coracle) | nothing | |

Every strike cracks a boat's bottom further, and a cracked bottom [leaks](Hull-Condition#leaks).

## The boats that run them

The [birch-bark canoe](Birch-Bark-Canoe) and the [coracle](Coracle) run rapids unharmed. They draw almost no water and ride over the rocks that batter everything else, as the canoes of the northern woodlands did on the rivers they were built for, and as Welsh coracles, floating in two or three inches of water, shot the rapids of rock-strewn rivers. Where a river runs too wild even so, pick the boat up and carry it around.

Heavy hulls, the [dugout](Dugout) and the [outrigger](Outrigger-Canoe), shrug off a few seconds of white water but not a long run. Light ones break within seconds: the [raft](Raft) comes apart, and the [currach](Hide-Currach) has the lath under its hide broken by the first rock.

Rapids harm a hull while [Hull damage](World-Settings#the-mechanics) is on. How the strikes are weighed is on [Hull condition](Hull-Condition#how-a-hull-takes-a-blow).
