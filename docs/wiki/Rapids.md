The mountain streams of Vintage Story 1.22 run down in white water. Every second a hull spends in it, the rocks strike it with 300 joules, a hard knock, again and again.

| Vessel | Chance of harm each second |
| --- | --- |
| [Coracle](Coracle) | 53% |
| [Raft](Raft), [reed boat](Reed-Boat) | 22% |
| [Hide currach](Hide-Currach) | 10% |
| [Outrigger canoe](Outrigger-Canoe) | 2% |
| [Dugout](Dugout) | 1% |
| [Sailboat](Sailboat) | under 1% |
| [Birch-bark canoe](Birch-Bark-Canoe) | none |

An oak raft that runs a stretch of rapids lasts about a quarter of a minute.

## The canoe that runs them

The [birch-bark canoe](Birch-Bark-Canoe) is the only vessel that runs rapids unharmed. It draws almost no water and rides over the rocks that batter everything else, as the canoes of the northern woodlands did on the rivers they were built for. Where a river runs too wild even so, pick the canoe up and carry it around.

Heavy hulls, the [dugout](Dugout) and the [outrigger](Outrigger-Canoe), shrug off a few seconds of white water but not a long run. Light ones, the [coracle](Coracle), the [raft](Raft) and the [reed boat](Reed-Boat), break within seconds.

Rapids harm a hull with [Dangerous oceans](World-Settings#dangerous-oceans) on or off. How the strikes are weighed is on [Hull condition](Hull-Condition#how-a-hull-takes-a-blow).
