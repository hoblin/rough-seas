Rough Seas builds its sea, its weather and its hulls from real measurements and the models scientists and engineers use, and bends them only where the game needs it. The other pages say what you meet at sea; these say why it is so, what each part stands on and where we bent it.

| [The science of the waves](The-Science-of-the-Waves) | waves and how they break, short waves, and the surf on the shore: over the bed, run-up, the sheet, faces, foam and clear water |
| [The science of a body in the water](The-Science-of-a-Body-in-the-Water) | how the waves move a hull, a swimmer, driving a hull with paddles, poles and oars, the current that carries it and the rope that holds it |
| [The science of the hull](The-Science-of-the-Hull) | knocks and rams, what a blow, a cut and a breaker do to a hull, floating, leaks, riding the swell, and how each vessel is built |
