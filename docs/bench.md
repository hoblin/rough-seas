# Testing on the bench

Read before testing in the game, together with `bin/game help`. The bench's defaults carry most of the old rakes: the player arrives flying, restarts save the world, unridden vessels hold still, temporal effects are off, every wait has a timeout. What follows is what no tool can carry.

- To look at anything, fly there: `bin/scene look ID` from any side (`0 6 0.01` looks down from above the water's tint), `/bridge fly` anywhere. Never stand in water or build posts and slabs.
- Leave the desktop alone: no real keys, no window moves, no new monitors or outputs. The bench places its own window.
- Modifier clicks don't reach an unfocused client: use `/bridge interact` and `/bridge dismount`. On a block, a modifier held by `bin/game press` reaches it: `(bin/game press ShiftLeft 1500 &); sleep 0.3; bin/game click right 150` lays dry grass as a fire base, as Shift + right-click does.
- `/bridge hold` puts a single item in the hand: a test that takes several from a stack (firewood onto a pile) gives a full stack with `/giveitem` and picks its slot with `bin/game slot`.
- `/we` takes coordinates relative to the map centre, fills with the held block and needs creative; `/we delete` removes the marked blocks, while `/we clear` only forgets the marks. `/time set` and `/time add` move storms, and `/time set` only ever moves the clock forward, so set the hour before bringing a storm, not after. `/debug lntest spawn` strikes along the body's yaw, not the camera's.
- Bring a storm to the player rather than flying to it: `/bridge await [TYPE] [WARMTH]` moves time on, up to a year, until a storm of the model covers the player where they stand. A place gets the storms its climate brews: a dry subtropical coast sees gales only in winter, and squalls need warmth and rain, so when a year brings none, fly once to where `/seas storms` lists them and wait there.
- Our own diagnostics: `/seas here` and `.seas` give the same reading from the server's model and from the map the client received; `/seas ocean` shows the world's ocean map at the player, how the built world calibrated it, whether the water here is built, and the fetch upwind. Every weather event a region takes is logged with the rain and warmth at its centre (`grep -a 'weather event'`).
- After a `/tp`, `~` commands use the client's stale position: `bin/game arrive` first (the bridge's own flights wait by themselves).
- `/bridge sea` reads the raw ocean map, which under Terra Prety is high over dry flats too; `/bridge find saltwater-still-7` finds real sea water. It fails in a world with no open sea in its generated regions; open another world. A new world has a new seed, so coordinates from another world point elsewhere.
- Another mod on the bench: put its zip in `$BENCH_DATA/Mods` and take it out after. The dedicated server's world takes the bench preset when `serverconfig.json` names `"PlayStyle": "roughseastest"` and a new save file.
- Spectators don't pick: hover text and entity selection need creative or survival.
- To see a handbook page: `bin/game hotkey handbook` opens the handbook, then `bin/game link handbook://PAGECODE` and `bin/game shot FILE gui`; `bin/game scroll N` brings the end of a long page into view. The list stays empty while the handbook builds its pages, for half a minute after the world loads, and when a link opens a closed handbook: wait, open it by hotkey, and search again.
- The wiki's recipe images come from the handbook: `bin/scene recipes docs/wiki/images/recipes` retakes them all. A changed recipe is retaken this way, and a new one gets its line in that command's list first.
- Clean up after each test with `bin/scene clear` instead of opening new worlds; it removes entities only.
- Launching an existing world with `--playStyle` crashed the client on reopen (#24); `bin/client` passes the preset only to new worlds. A crash on a stable build is ours until a vanilla run (`BENCH_MODS=`) reproduces it, and the log names the failing entity.
- `pkill -f "bin/game …"` matches, and kills, your own shell.
- bin/client turns vsync off: a hidden window with vsync gets one frame a second. On Hyprland (Lua config) with `BENCH_WORKSPACE` set in `mise.local.toml`, it also puts the game on that workspace (on `BENCH_MONITOR`, when set) and hands focus back.
- Board a vessel (`/bridge mount ID`) to work on it and leave with `/bridge dismount` (a `/tp` while seated takes the vessel along); tie it with `/bridge tie`, read its ropes with `/bridge ropes`.
- `/weather setprecip` overrides all our rain, storms' included, and the world keeps it: `bin/scene`'s calm scenes set it to 0. `/weather setprecipa` hands the rain back to our weather.
- Storms pass a spot within minutes of game time: `/time stop` holds them while you look, and stills everything else at sea with them, as it stills vanilla's swaying plants: the drawn waves, waves striking a hull, lightning, rain into a bilge, leaks. `/time resume` before watching a vessel ride or take the sea, `/bridge approach` puts you outside one, facing it. Look at the sky and the sea in spectator (`/gamemode spectator`), which draws no player; it picks nothing, so return to creative to board.
- `.surface waves|foam on|off` leaves the sea's waves or foam out, for a side-by-side shot or a frame-time comparison. `.surface reload` recompiles the shader includes after `dotnet build RoughSeas`, without a restart; `.surface trace` logs every vessel's pose each frame, to tell a jump in our pose from a stall in the recording. `.surface samplers` lists every shader program's samplers by the unit the engine numbers them on, and where the height map is read: after a shader change, vanilla's numbers must stay as without the mod.
- `/bridge force N` finds the roughest open sea where the wind blows at Beaufort N. Seated, it gives the `/tp` that takes the vessel along: the server can't move a vessel its rider's client steers.
- A move far across the world stalls the server while it loads and generates the chunks there, for up to a minute: a tick of that length in `server-main.log` is the game's, not ours (`/seas budget` shows how much of it was our code). Never wait a guessed pause. `bin/game do` waits after every bridge flight until the server's debug log says the chunks around the player loaded; after a plain `/tp`, fly there with `/bridge fly` instead. Record over a long take: a recording caught while chunks load looks like our stutter.
- The bench's clock stands still. Time afloat (a reed boat soaking, a currach's grease wearing off) passes with `/time add 24 hours`, a day at a step; anything metered as it runs (a leak, a seep, rain into a bilge, waves striking) needs `/time resume`, and `/time stop` after. A `/time add` brings the rain and leaks of the hours it adds, but not their waves or lightning, which no tick lived through; `/time speed 17000`, sleep's pace, brings all of it. A player left over water after a restart has sunk to the seabed: `/bridge fly ~ 111 ~` before spawning a vessel at them.
- `/bridge set ID tree/key VALUE` reaches into a tree, and a double or float it finds stays one: `roughseas:vessel/cracks/portside 0.4` for a crack at a stage (a hull gets its `cracks` tree on its first harm: strike it once), `roughseas:vessel/lashing 0.4` for a raft, `roughseas:vessel/float/lashing 0.4` for an outrigger's float, `roughseas:bilge/litres 450` for a swamped one. A hull draws the wound its crack has reached at once: set each place to another stage and look at her from both sides and from her seat. `/bridge strike ID J starboardside` on an outrigger meets her float.
- `bin/scene seats` seats the player in every seat of every vessel and shoots each from the side, in third person; `/bridge mount ID SEAT` takes one seat. The camera mode outlives a shot and a restart: after third-person work, `cyclecamera` back to first person before `look` or `photo`.
- `bin/scene strokes` rows or paddles each vessel forward and backward and films one stroke from the front, the side and behind; it empties the player's hand first, since a held oar is drawn over the rower's arms.
- An accessory goes on with `/bridge interact ID interact ctrl BOX`, BOX being its selection box. A basket attaches only with its type: `/giveblock stationarybasket-east 1 <player> {type:"reed"}`.
- A bowl held over the sea fills from it, and a full bowl bails nothing: `/clearinv` and a fresh bowl before a bailing test, aimed into the boat.
- An open handbook pauses the game, and a paused client answers no `entity` request: `bin/game close`.
- The bench is muted by the master volume only, so the engine still loads and resolves every sound: a file it can't find logs `Audio File not found` in `client-main.log`. `BENCH_SOUND=100 bin/client` starts it audible, for someone listening. Check sounds by their `[sounds]` debug lines (sounds on a hull or a wave in the server's log; the open sea's bed, whitecaps, a vessel's loops and every splash of a crest in the client's), `.sounds shore` (how the water's edges around the player lap or break) and `.sounds vessels` (what each vessel near the player plays). Every sound the client starts, fades or stops, ours and vanilla's, logs a `[sound]` line in `client-debug.log` with its file, type, distance and volume, and `bin/game state` lists those playing now: `grep -a '\[sound\]' "$BENCH_DATA/Logs/client-debug.log"` shows what switched on, when and how loud. Hearing them is the maintainers'.
- When something fails twice by hand, build the tool before the third try.
- Sand and gravel fall: filled at sea level with `/we`, they sink into the sea and take whatever stands on them. Build a beach of soil (`game:soil-medium-normal`) or stone.
- `/bridge mount` seats the player without asking `CanSit`: an unfinished vessel's refusal is tested with a click, `bin/game hover ID` then `bin/game click right`. `/bridge set ID roughseas:build/STAGE N` jumps a build to a stage for a look, and starts one on a spawned, finished vessel: the stages not set count as not begun. A jump redraws her but doesn't reweigh her: to read her mass or draft at a stage, lay its last piece by hand.
