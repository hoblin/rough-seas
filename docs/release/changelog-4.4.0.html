4.4: the sea reaches the shore, and you float in it.

  • Surf on the shore. As the water shallows the waves grow higher and turn to face the shore, then break: they spill on a gentle beach and plunge on a steep one. Each broken wave runs up the beach as a thin sheet and slides back, and the sand it wets stays dark. A bar or a reef far out breaks the waves too, and behind it the sea is calmer. More: The look of the sea.
  • Surf foam is back. It rides the front of each breaking wave and runs up the beach with the swash, white on the front and breaking into lace behind.
  • Waves against a face. A cliff, a quay, a wall you built: a wave that doesn't break stands up against it, and one that breaks throws spray high up it.
  • A finer sea near you. Close to you the water is divided finer than a block, so crests round off and short waves show on the swell. A graphics card below OpenGL 4.0 draws the same waves a block at a time.
  • Water under the beach. The sea's water lies under the shore as far up as the waves run, further in a storm. Dig down to its level and the hole fills. More: Water under the beach.
  • You float by your body. In the water you rise, lie and heave with the waves by the water you displace. Armour and the tools in your hotbar weigh you down, below about 6 m you sink, and a crest breaking over you drives you under. More: Swimming.
  • Boats take the waves where they are. A hull behind a bar is hit softer, and the surf sounds as big as the sea coming in. Vessels at sea now take a wave every wave period: in 4.3 they took about one in ten.
  • Older game versions. Rough Seas now runs on Vintage Story 1.22.0 and later.
