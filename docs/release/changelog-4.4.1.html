A fix for two things players met in 4.4.0.

  • The world no longer goes invisible beside a shader mod. A mod that replaces the game's terrain shaders, SheyderMod 2.1.1 among them, could leave everything but the grass undrawn. Where our wet sand or waves don't fit a replaced shader, that shader now draws as its mod wrote it, and client-main.log says what was left out.
  • No crash beside a charred dugout when a player joins, respawns or comes into range. Chips still fly on every blow.

Thanks to Annastasija and PapitoPapz for the reports. SheyderMod 2.1.1 is now on the list of tested mods: Beside other mods.
