Hulls now take knocks the way real ones do. A boat is struck only when it truly hits something solid, by the speed it loses into it, the way the game works out a fall. Everything aboard counts: the people with their gear and armor, the empty baskets, crates and chests, and the water in the bilge, which sloshes rather than hitting as one. A lagging server no longer breaks a boat on open water.

For modders, the wiki now has a page per mechanic, and a guide to building vessels that ride our sea: For modders. What those pages document we keep stable under semantic versioning.

Shipworks+ made itself compatible with Rough Seas, and it is now on the list of tested mods. Its ships don't yet rise and roll with our drawn swell; that's on our list: Beside other mods.
