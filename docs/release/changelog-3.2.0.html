3.2 gives everyone on a server one sea, and makes calm water sound like calm water.

  • No more creaking in the shallows. Standing or swimming in shallow water played a loud creak and knock, recorded inside a boat at anchor, and it lingered after you left the water. It's gone. A calm sea is quiet now but for the splash of each passing wave, at your feet or at your hull, as loud as the wave is high.
  • The shore laps with the game's own sound again, by how much shallow water lies around you.
  • The sea's roar rises from silence as the wind picks up, instead of starting at full strength. The slap of a wave on your hull sits just over its splash, so a calm no longer rings with loud slaps.
  • The same waves for every player. Every client draws the same crests at the same moment, so a crewmate sees the wave your boat rides.
  • One clock at sea. Waves coming aboard, rain into a bilge, leaks, lightning, a reed boat soaking and a currach's grease all keep the world's time. They stand still while time stands, and race while you sleep: a boat afloat near you lives through the night with you, so a leaky one left by the shore may be full of water in the morning.

For modders: ISeaWeather.NowHours gave way to ISeaWeather.Clock, whose Hours is the old value, and IDrawnSea.Seconds gives the waves' time, the same on every client. In config/soundscape.json a hull's splash is water.splash, the slaps take a volumePerMetre, and the lapping loop and the shore's lapping keys are gone. What changed: For modders.
