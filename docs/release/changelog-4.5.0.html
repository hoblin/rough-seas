4.5: the sea pushes, and the boats carry their load loose.

  • Holds in place of containers. The dugout, the outrigger, the bark canoe and the currach carry cargo loose on their floors, the way real boats are loaded: at its true size a basket fits inside none of them. Right-click the floor of a hold to open it. Its largest stacks lie there as small piles, ingots in a stack, planks in a pile, growing as the hold fills. The reed boat's basket stands at its true size. More: Holds.
  • Your containers move into the holds. A basket or chest already aboard one of these boats is emptied into its holds when the world loads, and then put there itself. Nothing is lost.
  • The water pushes a hull. A fast boat running before a swell is pushed down a wave's face and rides it toward the shore. A slow one lets the faces pass under it. More: How the waves move a hull.
  • Swimming by force. You stroke with a swimmer's strength, and the current carries you.
  • Moorings hold by the rope. A moored boat comes taut with a jerk, bounces back and lies working in the swell, and the rope is judged by the hardest pull it takes. More: Mooring.
  • Knots tied by hand. Binding rope or cord into a vessel, sewing on a hide or binding up a lashing, both hands tie a knot.
  • A wedge split cracks and no longer sounds like a falling tree.
  • The sea's background sound is off for now: the open sea, the surf and the whitecaps' hiss didn't match the water you see, and come back from real recordings. The shore laps with the game's own sound, rowing plays at the game's volume, and every wave at a hull, every breaker and every knock still sounds.
  • The logo is the mod's icon in the game's mod list.
  • Vintage Story 1.22.4 or later.
