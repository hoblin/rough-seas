4.2: soak your laths without a barrel.

  • Laths soaked under stones. Tie a stack of dry laths with loose stones from the ground into a bundle and lay it on the bottom of any water, still or flowing: in two days the laths are wet, with no barrel and no saw. Untie it in the crafting grid to get the laths and every stone back. The barrel still soaks them too. More: Recipes.
  • The outrigger's washstrakes run the new dugout hull from end to end, and hold more water out. More: Outrigger canoe.
