A fix for two things players met in 2.0.0, both from one cause: the texture our waves read from shifted vanilla's shader slots.

  • Beside SheyderMod, leaves and plants no longer turn black past a few blocks.
  • Clouds no longer vanish from the sky in patches shaped like the sea around you.

Thanks to everyone who reported them. SheyderMod 2.0.0 is now on the list of tested mods: Beside other mods.
