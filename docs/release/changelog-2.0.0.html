2.0 "Undercurrent" moves Rough Seas under vanilla. Our weather now drives the game's own wind, rain and weather events, so other weather mods build on it instead of fighting it. We tested it beside seven mods, and the wiki says how each pair plays together: Beside other mods.

Waves grow from the wind, the open water and the time it has blown, so a big lake raises a sea of its own. Gales and hurricanes are born where and when real ones are. The coordinates readout shows the wind where you stand. The dugout and the outrigger build from any tree, other mods' trees too, and the bark canoe from birch, elm or spruce bark.

Switched off for now: capsizing, which comes back with heel in 2.1, and the surf's foam on the beach.

For server owners: the server alone works out the sea and the weather now, and the world's own ocean is used as it is. Old worlds load as they were; a birch-bark canoe stays a birch-bark canoe.
