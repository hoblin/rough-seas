4.1: you choose how hard the sea is, one mechanic at a time.

  • A setting for each mechanic. Water in the bilge, hull damage, lightning, mooring strain, currents, rower fatigue and the pole's depth each have a world setting of their own, all on by default and changeable in a world already made. Turn one off and the sea keeps its weather, but that mechanic leaves your game. They replace Dangerous oceans: a world made before 4.1 gives every setting its value on first load. More: World settings.
  • The dugout's ends are hollowed round inside, as burnt and hewn, through every stage, and closed outside with no gap. The hull runs the log's full 5 m. More: Dugout.
