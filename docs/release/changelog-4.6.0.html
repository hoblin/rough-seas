4.6: other mods' boats take our sea, and the water lets you out of it.

  • Shipwright. Its ten boats are vessels of ours: they ride the swell, take the sea and the current, ship water and are bailed, crack and flood, and tie to a stake. Each weighs what a real craft of its size and build weighs. Tested with Shipwright 1.5.1. More: Shipwright.
  • Walkable Entities. Standing on a boat's deck, you rise, fall and roll with it on our swell. Tested with Walkable Entities 1.0.44. More: Walkable Entities.
  • Wading. Until the water reaches your chest you walk and jump as on land, so a jump takes you up a one-block step out of the water. The water drags you by the part of you it covers. More: Wading.
  • Swimming by power. Your stroke pulls hardest from a standstill, so you are under way within a second or two.
  • Every part of a vessel can be aimed at. Seats, holds and the paddle and pole stowage were hidden inside the hull on several vessels. Look down into the vessel along its gunwale until the look reads Paddle and pole to stow one. A stowed paddle or pole lies centred on the vessel.
  • Every text translated, the hold's "Open" hint and the coracle's first build stage among them.
  • Lighter on the machine. The surf, the water shader and the shore water do their work once: about a third less time spent in the mod, and half the memory churn.
