⚠️ Release candidate 2. For testers. Use a test world; a world started on an RC may not carry over to 4.0.

What changed since rc.1:

  • The dugout's build. The axe stops when you let go, the blank no longer jumps, the bark strips along the whole trunk, a fire takes time to light and burns whoever stands in it, and the char is hewn out blow by blow.
  • Launching. You push a heavy vessel into the water on foot. A reed boat can be built anywhere.
  • Laying down. A vessel lies where you lay it, and no longer jumps or sinks into the ground.
  • Poling. Each push gathers way, astern has its own stroke, and a warning shows when the pole doesn't reach the bottom. Seated poling in first person keeps the view ahead clear.
  • Splitting poles. You drive each wedge on its own, blow by blow. Poles and laths get new icons.
  • Held work. Vanilla's progress bar shows on lashing and stripping bark, and no longer sticks on screen.
  • The reed boat is bound in the colours of dried plants.
  • The handbook is rewritten, its links work in every language, and every translation is corrected.
